Platform Features

Everything your team needs to move fast.

🎙
Offline Transcription

Vosk-powered ASR runs entirely on your machine. No API calls, no cloud uploads. Works with WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, and WebM.

⚡
Instant Summaries

Extractive NLP distils hours of conversation into the sentences that actually matter. Keywords, highlights, and decisions — instantly.

✅
Action Item Detection

Pattern-based extraction finds "will do", "should", and assignment language. Action items land straight on the kanban board.

📋
Kanban Board

Drag tasks through todo → in progress → done. Priority, assignee, deadline, labels, and notes on every card. Scoped to your role.

🏢
Org Hierarchy

Nested departments, manager assignments, and team-scoped visibility. Invite codes control who joins your workspace.

📊
Analytics & Reports

Dashboard with meeting volume trends, keyword frequency, action item completion rates, and team-level breakdowns. Weekly PDF export.

🔒
Confidentiality Controls

Public, internal, or password-protected confidential meetings. Admins control downgrade. Every action is logged to the audit trail.

🔍
Full-Text Search

FTS5-powered search across titles, summaries, and full transcripts. Tag filtering and keyword browsing. Role-scoped results.
